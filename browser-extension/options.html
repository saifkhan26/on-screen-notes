Send to On-Screen Notes

Right-click a picture, GIF or video and choose Send to On-Screen Notes, or pick one with the picker (the toolbar button, or its key below). It lands on the slide you have open.

Checking…

Connect asks On-Screen Notes to let this browser in: press Connect in the app when it asks. The app must be running, with Receive from the browser on in its settings.

The extension needs to read pictures on the sites you send them from, and to reach the app on this computer.

They work on web pages. Click one to change it, then press the new keys — Esc keeps the old one, Backspace takes it off. Keys with Alt or Ctrl work anywhere on a page; a plain key only while you aren't typing.

Browser shortcuts are set in the browser instead, and work even while the address bar has the keyboard. Every action is there, with none set.

The same number as browser_link_port in the app's config (47823 unless changed).
